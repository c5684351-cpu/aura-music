Aura Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Aura is a music app for listening to original songs and short song highlights ("Shorts"). This policy explains what information the Aura app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have. It applies to the Aura app for Android and to the Aura music library website that the app downloads songs from.

In short

  • Aura has no accounts and no sign-in. You never give us your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Aura has no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • Your likes, listening history and settings are stored only on your phone. We never receive them.
  • Aura downloads songs and cover images from a website hosted by GitHub. As with any website, GitHub receives technical information such as your IP address when files are downloaded.
  • We do not sell or share any information about you.

1. Information we do not collect

We (the developer of Aura) do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. Aura does not ask for, read or upload your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera or files. Aura does not use advertising identifiers and does not include any advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media software.

2. Information stored only on your device

To make the app work and to personalise it for you, Aura saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else:

  • Songs and Shorts you like.
  • Genres and artists you choose on the "taste" screen.
  • How many times you play or skip each song. Aura uses this only on your phone to suggest songs on the Home screen.
  • Your playback settings, such as play order (in order, shuffle or same genre) and repeat.
  • A copy of the song list, so the app opens quickly and works offline.
  • Copies of songs and cover images you have played, so they play without an internet connection and without downloading them again.

How to delete it: you can remove saved songs at any time in Aura under Settings > Saved on this phone > Clear. You can remove all Aura data by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings (Settings > Apps > Aura > Storage > Clear data) or by uninstalling the app.

3. Internet connections

Aura connects to the internet only to download the song list, songs and cover images from the Aura music library at c5684351-cpu.github.io. This website is hosted by GitHub, Inc. When your phone downloads a file, GitHub's servers receive standard technical information that every website receives, such as your IP address, the time of the request and basic device and browser details. Aura does not add any information about you to these requests. GitHub handles this information under its own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement. We do not receive these server logs.

The typefaces, icons and artwork used in the app's design are built into the app and are not downloaded from any other service.

4. Permissions and device features

  • Internet access: to download songs, cover images and the song list, as described above.
  • Background playback (foreground service and wake lock): to keep music playing when the screen is off or you use other apps, and to show playback controls in the notification area and on the lock screen. These controls show the song title, artist and cover image. They only control playback.
  • Audio focus and headphone detection: Aura uses Android's standard audio features to pause the music during a phone call, to lower the volume briefly for notification sounds, and to pause when headphones are unplugged or Bluetooth headphones are turned off, so music does not suddenly play from the phone's speaker. Aura only checks whether audio devices are connected at that moment. It does not store, record or send any information about your devices.

Aura does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

5. Sharing and selling of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information about you, because we do not collect any. We do not use any third-party advertising or analytics services.

6. Data security and retention

Information that Aura keeps on your device is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access under Android's normal security rules. It stays there until you clear it or uninstall the app. Downloads from the Aura music library use encrypted HTTPS connections.

7. Children's privacy

Aura is a general audience music app and is not directed at children under 13. Aura does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Aura, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. The information on your device is fully under your control, as described in section 2. For any question about your rights, please contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how Aura handles information, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app's release notes.
